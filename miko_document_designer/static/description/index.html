Document Designer

Design every document you print - quotation, invoice, purchase order, delivery slip - from one screen. Installs on its own: no other app is pulled in.

One real quotation, printed by Odoo 19 with the design applied. Nothing here is a mock-up.

Odoo stops at the logo. This is the rest.

Odoo's own layout screen gives you a logo, two colours and a font. Everything a printed document actually needs after that - how tight the rows are, whether lines are numbered, whether the product is pictured, what is stamped across the page, what is printed behind it and what is attached after it - has no home in Odoo at all. It has one here.

What you get

The line table, finally under control

Striped rows in your own colour. Numbered lines. Compact or roomy row height. Horizontal rules, a full grid, or none. A coloured header row. Section headers, notes and subtotals are never numbered or striped as if they were products.

The product, pictured

The product image beside each line, at the size you choose. The picture is matched to the line it belongs to and checked before it is printed: if the document and the record do not agree, no picture is printed at all rather than the wrong one.

Watermark, letterhead, terms

A text or image watermark on every page, at the angle, size and opacity you set. Your letterhead PDF printed behind every page - merged, not flattened, so the text stays selectable and searchable. Your terms and conditions appended after the last page of every document.

The total in words, and where to pay

The total written out in the document's own currency and language, and a bank details block. Where a document names its own bank account, that is the account printed - never a different one from the company's list.

Rules, for the documents that are different

One customer, or one kind of document, can print differently from the rest: its own stamp, its own layout, no letterhead, no appended terms. A rule carries only what it changes, so nothing else about that document moves.

Built to be uninstallable

Everything Odoo already owns - logo, colours, font, paper format, header, footer, company address, layout - is written straight back to the company record, not copied into a private one. Your existing reports stay correct, other modules reading those fields get the truth, and removing this module leaves your branding exactly as you set it.

Installing it changes nothing. Not one printed document moves until you design one, and a single switch returns every document to stock Odoo without losing a setting.

Tested, on every version it claims

83 automated tests, run on a real Odoo 16, 17, 18 and 19 install before each release, plus a real PDF rendered and read on each one. Depends on nothing but Odoo itself.
